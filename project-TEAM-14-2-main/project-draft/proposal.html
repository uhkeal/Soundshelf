SoundShelf

Problem/Data Domain

The topic we chose for our project was based on our shared interest in music, specifically in discovering music and connecting people through shared tastes.

Current music platforms such as Spotify and Apple Music rely heavily on algorithm-driven recommendations that prioritize already popular songs. This makes it harder for niche and emerging artists to gain visibility and limits how users explore music. Many users have expressed frustration with how difficult it is to find new and unique music on these platforms (Reddit discussion). Additionally, industry analysis shows that many smaller artists remain largely invisible because platforms are designed to promote familiar content rather than new discoveries (Hypebot article).

While platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, and TikTok attempt to support music discovery, they still focus heavily on trending or viral content. These systems do not provide users with enough control over how they search for music or interact with smaller artists. This creates a gap where users cannot easily explore music based on specific interests, moods, or community-driven recommendations. It is a top-heavy system that is non-beneficial to smaller artists.

App Description

Our app is designed for music listeners who want to discover new artists and explore different kinds of music. This app is for people who find mainstream music platforms frustrating because they often only recommend already popular music. An impact this app might have on minority groups is that it gives a space for new and underrepresented artists to be discovered.

The app would include an MP3 file upload feature, and an artist could upload an MP3 to share songs on the platform. This gives the independent artists chances to share their music and contribute to the growth of the platform. We would also include a tag-based song search feature. This means that users would be able to search songs based on different tags such as genre, mood, and style. This makes the music discovery process smoother and helps users to find the songs they might actually be interested in. Users can also create and save songs to their playlist. Lastly, they can rank songs based on their preferences and make it possible for good songs to gain visibility on the platform.

The main kind of information users will view will be the uploaded songs displayed in the app, with song name, artist, tags, and ranking. It solves the problem by creating an app that is more interactive and community-based compared to the mainstream music platforms. The app gives users more freedom in how they find music and gives smaller artists more chances to be noticed. By combining these features, the app makes it possible for a more engaging way to discover music.
